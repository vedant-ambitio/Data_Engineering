  2) THE INTERNSHIP REALITY FOR CLASS 9-12 STUDENTS
================================================================================

  The internship market for high school students (class 9-12, age 14-18)
  is VERY DIFFERENT from college internships. Here's the honest picture:

  THE HARSH TRUTH:
  ----------------
  - Most real internships (Google, Amazon, TCS, Infosys) require you to be
    in college — they don't accept high school students
  - ISRO, IIT, DRDO internships are for B.Tech/M.Tech students, not school
  - Internshala has 7,600+ internships but 95% are for college students
  - A 15-year-old searching "internship" will find mostly irrelevant results

  WHAT ACTUALLY EXISTS FOR CLASS 9-12:
  ------------------------------------

  TYPE 1: STRUCTURED PROGRAMS (best quality, limited spots)
    These are real programs designed specifically for school students.
    - ISRO YUVIKA — space science exposure for class 9 students
    - Plaksha Young Technology Scholars — engineering challenges
    - IISER Summer Outreach Camp — science research exposure
    - Ashoka Young Scholars — liberal arts exposure
    - Some IIT summer camps (not internships, but exposure programs)
    Count: ~10-15 programs nationally
    Mode: In-person (residential), 1-4 weeks
    Cost: Free to INR 5,000
    Availability: Summer only (May-June), competitive admission

  TYPE 2: VIRTUAL/REMOTE INTERNSHIPS (growing, mixed quality)
    Online platforms offering project-based work for school students.
    - Medicine Encompassed — writing/editing in biology/medicine
    - Building-U — multidisciplinary (R&D, marketing, coding)
    - Ladder Internships — curated internships for school students
    - LetsIntern — startup internships (some accept school students)
    - Virtual internship platforms (Forage, Bright Network)
    Count: ~20-40 at any time
    Mode: Remote, flexible hours
    Stipend: Mostly unpaid, some INR 2K-5K/month
    Quality: VARIES WIDELY — many are glorified certificate programs

  TYPE 3: AMBITIO PARTNER INTERNSHIPS (curated, vetted)
    Ambitio's own partnerships with companies/organizations.
    - Direct partnerships with EdTech, Finance, Marketing companies
    - Structured projects, real mentorship
    - Application via Ambitio internal form
    Count: Depends on Ambitio's partnerships team
    Mode: Remote/Hybrid
    Quality: High (Ambitio vets them)

  TYPE 4: PAID CERTIFICATE PROGRAMS (avoid these)
    "Internships" that charge INR 5,000-20,000 for a certificate.
    - No real work, no mentorship
    - Just watch videos + get a PDF certificate
    - The PDF spec explicitly says to EXCLUDE these
    - These are the ones the value-for-money filter catches

  REALISTIC NUMBERS:
  ------------------
    Internet listings (Internshala + curated):  50-65 listings
    Ambitio partner listings:                   TBD (from colleague)
    Certificate mills (excluded):               100+ exist, ALL filtered out
    ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
    TOTAL for Activity Finder:                  50-65 internet + partner TBD

  This is MUCH smaller than competitions (250-300) because:
    - Most companies legally can't hire under-18 for real work
    - Labour laws restrict what school students can do
    - The market is genuinely thin — not a scraping problem
    - No platform specializes in class 9-12 internships at scale

  WHY 50-65 INTERNET LISTINGS IS GOOD COVERAGE:
    - It's nearly ALL real high school internships available online
    - Internshala alone has 39 (the largest pool) — we capture all of them
    - Curated articles add 15-25 unique programs platforms miss
    - The PDF says "Content team reviews and approves each listing"
    - Quality > Quantity for internships
    - A student seeing 50 vetted internships is better than 500 spam ones
    - Pagination: 50 / 8 cards per page = ~6 pages — enough to browse
    - Partner listings from Ambitio will add more on top

  3) DATA SOURCES
================================================================================

  TWO CHANNELS (per PDF spec):

  CHANNEL 1: AMBITIO PARTNER (internal, not scraped)
    Source: Ambitio partnerships team provides structured data
    Data: Company name, title, industry, stipend, duration, mode, eligibility
    CTA: "Apply via Ambitio" (internal form, no external redirect)
    Quality: Highest (Ambitio vetted)
    Volume: 10-30 listings
    Update: As partnerships team adds new ones via CMS
    >> NEED FROM TEAM: partner listing data (CSV/JSON)

  CHANNEL 2: INTERNET LISTINGS (scraped + manually approved)

    There is NO single "BigFuture" equivalent for high school internships.
    Most platforms (Internshala, LinkedIn, Unstop) are built for college
    students — high school is an afterthought. The market is genuinely
    small: ~50-80 real internships exist for class 9-12 at any time.

    SOURCE RESEARCH (verified April 2026):

      Source                   HS listings  Scrapeable?  Notes
      ----------------------   -----------  -----------  --------
      internshala.com          ~39          Yes (Apify   Closest to single source.
                                            + GitHub     Pre-built scrapers exist.
                                            scrapers)    India-focused. Structured data.
      ladderinternships.com    13           Blog only    Editorial list, not a database.
      internhq.com             10-20        HTML         Mostly international, thin.
      letsintern.in            Few          JS-rendered  Mostly college-focused.
      gradsqr.com              Curated      Blog only    Just a list article.
      Skill India Digital      Gov portal   Unknown      Govt scheme, not listings.

    PRIMARY SOURCE: INTERNSHALA
      - 39 listings for "high school" keyword (most of any platform)
      - Pre-built scrapers: Apify scraper, 4+ GitHub repos, Bright Data
      - Structured data: title, company, stipend, duration, skills, mode
      - Filters: work-from-home, part-time, stipend range
      - Covers ~60-70% of what's available online for HS students

    SUPPLEMENTARY: CURATED ARTICLE SCRAPING
      - ladderinternships.com (13 programs), lumiere-education.com (10),
        admissionsight.com (15), questforsuccess.in (programs list)
      - These are blog articles with hand-picked programs — not databases
      - Scrape article pages, extract program names + URLs + details
      - Adds ~15-25 unique programs not on Internshala

    SCRAPING PLAN:
      Internshala (automated, weekly)     → ~35-40 filtered listings
      Curated articles (monthly scrape)   → ~15-25 unique programs
      ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
      Internet listings total             → ~50-65 listings
      + Ambitio partner listings          → TBD (from colleague)

    CTA: "Apply on official website" (opens program page in new tab)
    Quality: Medium (needs content team approval before going live)
    Update: Weekly for Internshala, monthly for curated articles

    IMPORTANT: Internet scraped listings go into a REVIEW QUEUE — they
    don't go live until content team approves using the value-for-money
    filter from the PDF spec.

  WHAT WE CAN SCRAPE vs WHAT WE CAN'T:

    Can scrape:                          Can't scrape (need from team):
    ─────────────────────────────────    ──────────────────────────────
    Internet listings from platforms     Ambitio partner listings
    Company names, stipends, modes       Internal application form routing
    Descriptions, eligibility            Value-for-money approval decision
    Deadlines, durations                 "Ambitio Partner" badge assignment

  IMPORTANT: Internet scraped listings go into a REVIEW QUEUE — they don't
  go live until content team approves. This is different from olympiads and
  competitions where scraped data goes directly to UI.
