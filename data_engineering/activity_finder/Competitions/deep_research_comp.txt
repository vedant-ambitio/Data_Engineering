# DEEP RESEARCH TASK — Student Competitions (Class 8–12, India / Online Only)

## YOUR ROLE
You are a research agent. Your job is to find real, verified, currently active
or upcoming competitions for high school students (Class 8–12 / ages 13–18)
that are either:
- Fully online / remote, OR
- Held physically inside India only (no international travel required)

Do NOT include competitions that require students to travel abroad for any round.
Do NOT include Olympiads (SOF, Silverzone, HBCSE, Unified Council, CREST, etc.)
— those are handled separately in the Olympiads section.

---

## PHASE 1 — MARKET RESEARCH

Before finding individual competitions, perform deep research to answer:

1. How many competitions exist in India / online for high school students
   across all domains? (Estimate: 200-400+ total, 80-120 with active
   registration at any given time)
2. What are the main DOMAINS?
   (STEM, coding, business, arts, writing, environment, social impact, etc.)
3. What are the TOP listing platforms for Indian high school competitions?
   Research: studentcompetitions.com, dare2compete.com, unstop.com,
   devpost.com, olympiad websites, scholasticworld.blogspot.com,
   contest360.in, olympiadtester.in
4. How many competitions currently have active registration windows RIGHT NOW?

Write a brief summary to: Competitions/competition_data/MARKET_RESEARCH.md
