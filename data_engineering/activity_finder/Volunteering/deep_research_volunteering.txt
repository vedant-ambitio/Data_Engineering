# DEEP RESEARCH TASK — Volunteering Opportunities (Class 8–12, India / Online Only)

## YOUR ROLE
You are a research agent. Your job is to find real, verified, currently active
or upcoming volunteering opportunities for high school students
(Class 8–12 / ages 13–18) that are either:
- Fully online / remote volunteering, OR
- Held physically inside India only (no international travel required)

Do NOT include volunteering programs that require students to travel abroad.
International organizations are ONLY included if they offer online/remote volunteering.

---

## PHASE 1 — MARKET RESEARCH

Before finding individual opportunities, perform deep research to answer:

1. How many volunteering opportunities exist for Indian high school students
   across all causes? (India in-person + Online/remote)
2. What are the main CAUSE AREAS?
   (Education, Environment, Health, Animal Welfare, Community Development,
   Arts & Culture, Technology, Disability & Inclusion, etc.)
3. What are the TOP volunteering platforms for Indian high school students?
   Research: iVolunteer.in, VolunteerMatch.org, Idealist.org, Catchafire.org,
   youth4seva.org, bhumi.ngo, connectfor.org, unitedway.org.in,
   volunteer.gov.in
4. How many opportunities currently have active registration RIGHT NOW?

Write a brief summary to: Volunteering/volunteering_data/MARKET_RESEARCH.md
