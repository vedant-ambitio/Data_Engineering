  2) VOLUNTEERING MARKET FOR CLASS 9-12 STUDENTS
================================================================================

  Unlike internships (companies hire you) or competitions (you compete for
  prizes), volunteering is donating time for free to help a cause. Students
  do it for college applications, skill building, and certificates.

  DISTRIBUTION BY CAUSE AREA (matching our 7 UI filters):

    Cause area                   % of market   Available opportunities
    --------------------------   -----------   ----------------------
    Education                    ~40%          30-45 (tutoring, mentoring, teaching)
    Social Impact                ~15%          13-20 (women empowerment, child welfare)
    Environment & Sustainability ~15%          13-20 (conservation, climate action)
    Health & Wellbeing           ~10%           8-13 (hospitals, mental health, accessibility)
    Disaster Relief & Community  ~10%           7-11 (relief drives, rural development)
    Animals & Wildlife            ~5%           6-10 (shelters, wildlife conservation)
    Arts & Culture                ~5%           6-10 (writing, museums, cultural projects)
    --------------------------   -----------   ----------------------
    TOTAL                        100%          83-129

  KEY NGOS & PROGRAMS (India):
    - Teach For India — Summer Fellowship, mentoring in low-income schools
    - Pratham — playful learning for 1M+ kids
    - Goonj — urban surplus into rural infrastructure
    - Bhumi — tutoring 500K+ kids across cities
    - Youth for Seva — multi-focus (education, health, environment)
    - iVolunteer.in — India's largest volunteer platform, 12 cause areas
    - ISPIICE — volunteer work for high school students
    - Kaya — community projects for under-18s

  KEY PROGRAMS (International / Online):
    - UN Online Volunteers — global tasks (translation, design, research)
    - Learn to Be — 1-on-1 online tutoring
    - Zooniverse — citizen science research from home
    - Smithsonian Transcription — digitize historical documents
    - Be My Eyes — help blind individuals via video app
    - Writing Wonders — letters/artwork for communities
    - Climate Leaders Fellowship — 12-week program with Stanford

                                                       writing, social media)
      Individual NGO sites     15-25      71-86%       Top-tier India (TFI, Goonj,
                                                       Pratham, ISPIICE, Kaya)

    DROPPED SOURCES (and why):
      Youth for Seva   — only 3-5 programs, covered by iVolunteer
      Bhumi.ngo        — single org, 1-2 programs, covered by individual NGOs
      UNV Online       — 18+ only, most class 9-12 can't apply

    WHY THESE 4:
      - iVolunteer.in = India's largest volunteer platform (17K+ vol/year)
      - VolunteerMatch = world's largest, has teen filter, richest data fields
      - Catchafire = unique skills-based projects (students build portfolio)
      - Individual NGOs = prestigious names students actually search for

    SCRAPING PLAN:
      iVolunteer.in (quarterly)             → ~40-50 India listings
      VolunteerMatch/Idealist (quarterly)   → ~20-30 international listings
      Catchafire (quarterly)                → ~10-15 skills-based listings
      Individual NGO sites (quarterly)      → ~15-25 top-tier programs
      ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
      Internet listings total               → ~85-120
      + Ambitio partner NGO listings        → TBD

    NOTE: All 3 platforms (iVolunteer, Idealist, Catchafire) are JS-rendered
    and need Puppeteer for scraping. Individual NGO sites are a mix.

    Refresh: Quarterly (volunteering programs are stable, not weekly churn)
    Both sources reviewed by content team before publishing.


  FIELD COVERAGE PER SOURCE (out of 21 scraped fields):

    We scrape in 3 stages per source:
      Stage 1: Listing page (one HTTP/Puppeteer request to list page)
      Stage 2: Detail page (click into each opportunity)
      Stage 3: Gemini enrichment (extract structured fields from text)

    Source                   Stage 1    Stage 2    Stage 3    Final
                             (listing)  (+detail)  (+Gemini)  coverage
    ----------------------   ---------  ---------  ---------  --------
    iVolunteer.in            12-14/21   15-17/21   17-19/21   81-90%

    VolunteerMatch has the richest fields (cause, skills, hours, teen
    filter, age, mode — all structured). iVolunteer.in is close behind.

    Individual NGOs vary — TFI has hours + certificate, others are sparse.

    WHAT'S FILLED ACROSS ALL SOURCES:
      Card view (6 fields):     ~85% (logo missing from platforms)
      Detail view (8 fields):   ~80% (certificate_provided often missing)
      Backend (7 fields):       ~85% (official_website sometimes missing)
      Overall average:          ~18/21 = ~83% with Gemini

    ALWAYS MISSING (2-3 fields):
      organization_logo    — platforms don't show logos. Use Clearbit API.
      certificate_provided — not tracked on most platforms. Gemini infers
                             from text when mentioned (e.g. TFI: "certificate
                             after 50+ hours").
      official_website     — platform URL vs NGO's own site. Gemini can
                             sometimes extract from description text.
