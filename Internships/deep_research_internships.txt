# DEEP RESEARCH TASK — Internships for High School Students (Class 8–12, India / Online Only)

## YOUR ROLE
You are a research agent. Your job is to find real, verified, currently active
or upcoming internships for high school students (Class 8–12 / ages 13–18)
that are either:
- Fully online / remote (work from home), OR
- Held physically inside India only (no international travel required)

Do NOT include internships that require students to travel abroad.
International companies are ONLY included if they offer remote internships.

Do NOT include full-time jobs or fellowships meant for college graduates.
Only include genuine internship programs that accept high school students.

---

## PHASE 1 — MARKET RESEARCH

Before finding individual internships, perform deep research to answer:

1. How many internships exist for Indian high school students (class 8-12)?
   NOTE: This market is genuinely small — most internship platforms (Internshala,
   LinkedIn, Unstop) are built for college students. Estimate: ~50-100 real
   internships at any given time.
2. What are the main DOMAINS?
   (Technology, Business & Marketing, Design & Creative, Research & Science,
   Writing & Journalism, Social Impact & NGO)
3. What are the TOP internship platforms for Indian high school students?
   Research: internshala.com, letsintern.in, internhq.com, unstop.com,
   linkedin.com, ladderinternships.com, gradsqr.com
4. How many internships currently have active applications RIGHT NOW?

Write a brief summary to: Internships/internship_data/MARKET_RESEARCH.md
